Impossible Together

This story follows Nyx, a guy who has a crush on his school president's best friend. But here's the twist.. the school president actually likes Nyx, while the best friend has someone else in mind. Who will Nyx choose? Is it even possible to be "together"? There are also plenty of side love stories to keep you hooked!

Obscure

Michelle enters college determined to become a great artist, inspired by her late grandmother. As a Multimedia Arts student, she believes passion is enough, but growing pressure, deadlines, and expectations lead to burnout. As doubt replaces confidence, she begins to question her abilities and dream, facing a harsh truth: what happens when what you love starts to break you?

Bouquet

A journey of staying and moving on through the chains of constricting life obstacles. Florence is a florist whose only goal is to suffice his late parents' struggles in depth of their rented land where their shop stands and Taro whose goal is to finally be free from his mother's grasp. The two go through life changing ups and down solving and unraveling mysteries in their lives and learning whether to move on or to stay.

One Year with You

If you had one more year with the person you love... would you change the past, or let destiny run its course? Rin Ayaka, a quiet and introverted girl, has always struggled to express her feelings - especially to the one who loved her the most, Shin Haruko. When their relationship falls apart because of her own mistakes, Rin is left drowning in regret. But fate isn't done with her yet. Given a chance to go back in time, Rin must relive the moments she took for granted and find the courage to love him the way he deserved - before time runs out again. A story about love, second chances, and the fight to make things right - even if it means rewriting your heart.

Fading Love's Memory

Jake is currently struggling with his true emotions. Although he has moved on to his previous crush, Noelle, he cannot ignore his feelings for Ziah. He had been suppressing his emotions for Ziah, but they wouldn't go away. Now he feels conflicted about his feelings for Ziah. He wonders if he can be honest with himself and make a decision that truly reflects his heart.
